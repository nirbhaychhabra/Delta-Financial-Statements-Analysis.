## 📌 Executive Summary

This project delivers a rigorous multi-year financial statement evaluation of **Delta Air Lines, Inc. (NYSE: DAL)** across fiscal years **2022–2024**, utilizing audited SEC Form 10-K disclosures. The model benchmarks operating performance, capital allocation efficiency, debt service coverage, and working capital dynamics following the post-pandemic commercial aviation recovery.

### Key Highlights & Financial Findings
- **Top-Line Growth:** Operating revenues expanded from **$58,048M (FY 2023)** to **$61,643M (FY 2024)** (+6.19% YoY), driven by premium passenger segment growth and corporate travel volume.
- **Cost Structure Pressures:** Salaries and employee-related costs grew to **$16,161M** (26.2% of revenue), while fuel expenses moderated to **$10,566M** (17.1% of revenue).
- **Liquidity Profile:** Current Ratio calculated at **0.37x** and Quick Ratio at **0.24x**, reflecting the airline industry's negative working capital cycle where ticket revenue is collected in advance of flight execution (air traffic liability).
- **Asset Productivity:** Total Asset Turnover reached **0.83x**, demonstrating high utilization of flight equipment and aircraft investments ($75,373M total assets).

```
│ • YoY $ Changes   │ • Common-Size %   │ • Liquidity: Current, Quick    │
│ • YoY % Changes   │ • % Total Assets  │ • Turnover: Asset, AR, Inv     │
│ • 3-Year Baseline │ • % Total Revenue │ • Profitability & Cash Flow    │
└───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Horizontal Analysis (Trend Evaluation)
- Tracks balance sheet line items across 2023–2024 with absolute variance ($ millions) and percentage variance.
- Identifies cash deployment strategies, including a **$1,127M liquidation** of short-term investments to fund fleet renewals and debt paydown.
- Evaluates cash and cash equivalents growth from **$2,741M** to **$3,069M** (+11.97%).

### 2. Vertical Analysis (Common-Size Statements)
- Standardizes all balance sheet accounts as a percentage of Total Assets ($75,373M in FY24).
- Standardizes all statement of operations line items as a percentage of Total Operating Revenue ($61,643M in FY24).
- Isolates fixed capital intensity: Property and equipment representing the dominant share of asset backing.

### 3. Ratio & Liquidity Analysis
- **Current Ratio:** `0.37x` (Current Assets / Current Liabilities)
- **Quick Ratio:** `0.24x` ((Cash + ST Investments + Net AR) / Current Liabilities)
- **Asset Turnover:** `0.83x` (Operating Revenue / Average Total Assets)
- **Cash Flow Dynamics:** Evaluates operating cash flow generation against capital expenditures (CapEx) for aircraft deliveries and debt amortization schedules.
